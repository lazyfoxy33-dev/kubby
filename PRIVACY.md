## Data Collection

Kubby **does not collect any personal data**.

- **Network**: Kubby does not connect to the internet and makes no network requests.
- **Files**: Kubby stores only file path references when you drag items in, including a local cache so the shelf is restored on the next launch. References remain until you remove them or clear the shelf, and file contents are never read, copied, or uploaded.
- **Screenshots**: When you turn on “Screenshot to Shelf”, Kubby accesses the screenshot folder you choose (via a security-scoped bookmark) only to add newly captured screenshots to the shelf as file references. Their contents stay on your Mac and are never uploaded.
- **Crash Reporting**: No third-party crash reporting service is integrated.
- **Analytics**: No analytics or tracking SDKs are integrated.

## Contact

For privacy-related questions, please open an issue at [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/lazyfoxy33-dev/kubby/issues).
