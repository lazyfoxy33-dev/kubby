## Data Collection

Kubby **does not collect any personal data**.

- **Network**: Kubby does not connect to the internet. It only talks directly to your own devices that you paired, over your local network (Bonjour/mDNS discovery + direct peer-to-peer connections). No server is involved.
- **Files**: When you put an item on the shelf, Kubby stores only a file path reference (plus a small local cache so the shelf is restored on the next launch). References stay until you remove them or clear the shelf. File contents are transferred between your paired devices **only when you actively take the item**, directly over your local network, and never through any server.
- **Screenshots**: When you turn on “Screenshot to Shelf”, Kubby accesses the screenshot folder you choose (via a security-scoped bookmark) only to add newly captured screenshots to the shelf as file references. Their contents stay on your Mac and are never uploaded.
- **Crash Reporting**: No third-party crash reporting service is integrated.
- **Analytics**: No analytics or tracking SDKs are integrated.
